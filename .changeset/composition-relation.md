`partOf` and `hasPart` are now a real [composition](https://typegraph.dev/ontology#composition) relation, realized by an edge kind that stores the containment. `partOf(part, whole, { via, partSide? })` and its mirror `hasPart(whole, part, { via, partSide? })` name that edge with `via`; orientation is inferred from the declared pair against the edge's endpoints, and when both orientations are admissible (a same-kind containment edge) the declaration names `partSide: "from" | "to"`. The realizing edge declares its whole-side cardinality as `"one"` or `"oneActive"` (`cardinality` when the part is the edge's `from`, `targetCardinality` when the part is its `to`), must be composition-exact (every endpoint pair it admits is a declared composition pair), and every composition edge that can accept a given part kind declares the same temporal population. `KindRegistry.compositionRelation()` exposes the derived `CompositionRelation` (pairs, realizing edge kinds, part side per pair) as the one owner every consumer reads; `via` and `partSide` travel through every representation of an ontology relation (compile-time, named, serialized, extension document, introspection).

Deleting a composition whole cascades leaf-first through its live parts closure, each part running through its own node-delete pipeline inside the whole's transaction, with restrict and cascade `onDelete` behaviors on non-composition edges honored per part. Composition edges never count as a `restrict` obstacle on either end, since the whole owns them. An instance-level cycle among reflexive composition edges is refused with `CompositionCycleError` (`COMPOSITION_CYCLE_DETECTED`) rather than looping or truncating. Resurrecting a soft-deleted whole restores the whole alone, and ending a whole's validity window is not a cascade; the cascade emits one operation-hook event, for the whole. See [Composition Cascade](https://typegraph.dev/limitations#composition-cascade).

`NodeDeletePolicy` gains `consumedEdgeIds` and `cascadeComposition`. `enforceNodeDeleteBehavior` filters a node's connected edges by `consumedEdgeIds` before applying the kind's `onDelete` behavior, and `nodeDeletePolicyRequiresPortablePath` is the one owner of the fused-versus-portable delete route, so a policy dimension the fused atomic delete cannot honor routes to the portable path instead of being silently ignored.

`planMerge` and `planMergeIncremental` report composition orphans in `MergePlanReview.compositionOrphans`: a part attached on the target to a whole the branch deletes, which applying the plan as trusted would leave dangling. `applyMergePlan` re-verifies the same finding inside the apply transaction under the per-graph write lock and refuses with `MergeCompositionOrphanError` (`MERGE_COMPOSITION_ORPHAN`). Merge apply deletes nodes through a policy-carrying member on the transaction runtime port with `cascadeComposition: false`, so the plan stays the authority over what apply deletes.

### Breaking

- `partOf` / `hasPart` require `via`; both factories take a third options argument. A `partOf` or `hasPart` without a realizing edge, or a `via` or `partSide` on any other meta-edge, is refused at registry build and at schema load with `ConfigurationError` (`ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_VIA_REQUIRED`, `ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_VIA_FORBIDDEN`, `ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_PART_SIDE_FORBIDDEN`). Both relations were inert before, so nothing that worked stops working; a persisted schema carrying the old three-part relation fails on load, and `getSchemaChanges` reports it before upgrade.
- A realizing edge with `"many"` or `"unique"` on the whole side is refused (`ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_CARDINALITY`), as is an edge admitting an endpoint pair that is not a declared composition pair (`ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_VIA_ENDPOINTS`), one edge kind declared in two orientations (`ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_VIA_MIXED`), and mixed `one` / `oneActive` populations for one part kind (`ONTOLOGY_COMPOSITION_POPULATION_MIXED`).
- Deleting a node that is a composition whole now deletes its parts. A consumer that relied on `partOf` / `hasPart` being documentation-only must move any relation that re-homes or retains parts on delete off composition; the [containment tiers](https://typegraph.dev/ontology#choosing-a-containment-tier) section says which relation each case belongs to.
