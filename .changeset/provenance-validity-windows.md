Provenance retraction now handles rows outside their validity window the way the composition audit does. A required composition part whose own window has ended or not begun is never believed, but it is live for `store.verifyConstraintFences()` while its row is not tombstoned, so retracting the support of its whole used to close the whole and leave the part attached to a tombstone, a `compositionExistence` violation, even though the retraction succeeded. A transition now closes every such part with its whole, at any depth, names it in `died`, and reopens it when it reopens the whole; it never appears in `survivedVia`, `unaffected` or `holding()`, and an optional part outside its window is still left alone. A reopen now restores each fact's stored validity window instead of starting a new one at the reopen and dropping its upper bound, so a fact or part outside its window stays outside it. A source outside its validity window supports nothing, and restoring one with `unRetract` now reopens nothing: it used to reopen the facts it alone held, and then close them again, so a failure caught partway through could leave one of them live with no support.
